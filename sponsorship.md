## Overview of Responsibilities  
Sponsorship is handled by the *ACL Sponsorship Director and by the sponsorship chairs of each *ACL event. Below we refer to the three of them as the Sponsorship team.
The Sponsorship Team is responsible for collecting funding from two kinds of sources: granting institutions and corporations. additionally, the sponsorschip team makes sure  hi-res and low-res logos from every sponsor are collected, for use in print publications and on the conference website. This is done by the event organizer who collects the logo for all the *ACL events and share it with the Publication Chairs and the Webmaster Chairs of each event.

**The following are the main responsibilities of the Sponsorship Team:**

**1. Early Preparation**  
**2. Sponsorship Outreach & Negotiation**  
**3. Benefit Fulfilment**  

An example spreadsheet for tracking sponsors is available [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14eczleuDQvYqIwGx5iiz9svW8vkNe2C_/edit?gid=1027845459#gid=1027845459).

### 2. Sponsorship Outreach & Negotiation 

This responsibility focuses on contacting and collecting funding from two kinds of sources: granting institutions and corporations. Most of the budget comes from the recurrent sponsors which are contacted by the Sponsorship Director.

For granting institutions, the lead time of institution grant is approximately 1 year. A formal proposal is usually required. Many funding institutions are unwilling to fund a conference more than once. Examples:
+ National Science Foundation
+ Sloan Foundation
 
Corporations must be approached several times:

+ the first time more than a year in advance, to ensure that the sponsorship is included on the budget for the next year;
+ six or so months before the conference, to re-awaken interest and stress the advertising potential
+ three months in advance, to handle the funds

### 3. Benefit Fulfilment

This responsibility focuses on delivering promised visibility and maintaining relationships with sponsors. The event organizer takes care of this activity.

The sponsorship team is responsible for obtaining hi-res and low-res logos from every sponsor, for use in print publications and on the conference website. Advertising that corporations may be offered:

+ Logos on proceedings and fliers, etc.
+ Logos on banners and signs at conference and social events
+ Logos on freebies and t-shirts
+ Short statements at reception, opening, etc.

Corporate sponsors have very tight restrictions on the use of their logos. Last-minute sponsors may not be able to get all the advertising promised.

### 4. Recruitment Network

The Gold, Diamond, and Platinum Sponsors are entitled to have access to the Recruitment Network, namely the list of participants who are on the market. To this end, the following steps are taken:

+ During registration, ACL 2025 participants are given the possibility to declare whether they want:
     a.	 some of their data (professional name, preferred name, affiliation, pronouns, country, and email) to be listed in the Conference Participants list and 
     b.	If they are interested in employment opportunities and agree to have their email shared with the sponsors

The event organizer takes care of sharing the data in a); 

+ The ACL sponsorship chairs create a Google Form to collect the CVs -- following the sample given to them by the ACL sponsorship director --  and invite all the ACL 2025 participants who expressed interested in employment opportunities and agree to have their email shared with the sponsors (b above). 
+ The sponsorship chairs share with the Gold+ sponsors, the excel file generated out the form and the directory containing the CVs, and, upon request by the sponsors, send them the zipped directory containing all CVs.
